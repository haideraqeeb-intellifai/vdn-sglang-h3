Verification & method

  • Both MP4 files passed complete video and audio decoding.
  • Both contain 243 H.264 frames at 736 × 1280 and 24 FPS.
  • Both contain 32 kHz stereo AAC audio and last exactly 10.125 seconds.
  • Runtime progress confirmed exactly four denoiser forwards per generation.
  • Runtime selected hybrid_window_attn_h3; SageAttention was not installed in the execution path.

Decoded video hashes match between runs: yes. Decoded audio hashes match: no.

The same seed was used for both runs. Deterministic CUDA algorithms were not enforced. The matching decoded video hash establishes bit-identical decoded video for these two samples; audio nondeterminism was not investigated.
